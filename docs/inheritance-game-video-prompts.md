# 传承之路 · 视频关键词

角色扮演主线:你是一名非遗传承人。两个场景,每个场景三段视频。

**每段 4–6 秒。** 播放时机写在每段下面。

**统一风格(所有段共用,不然接起来跳):**
```
Cinematic, shallow depth of field, film grain, natural light.
Muted warm palette. Slow camera — no fast cutting, no whip pans.
No text, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no deformed faces,
no extra limbs, no slow-motion.
```

## 场景一 · 新疆戈壁 · 维吾尔族十二木卡姆

### 1-A 「选择」用的环境镜头(出现在两个选项之前)

```
A vast Gobi desert in Xinjiang at golden hour. Cracked earth and gravel
stretching to a low ridge, heat shimmer on the horizon. A single figure
stands small in the middle distance, facing away, a shoulder bag at his side.
Wind moves dust across the ground.
Cinematic wide shot, very slow push-in, shallow depth of field, film grain,
muted ochre and grey. No text, no watermark.
```

### 1-B 「选对」· 文化活着

```
Late afternoon inside a low earthen house in Xinjiang. Three musicians sit on
a carpet: a rawap and a satar player, and a singer with a hand drum. The singer
sings with his eyes closed, head tilted back; the drum skin vibrates under his
palm. A boy of about ten sits cross-legged at the edge, watching the players'
hands, lips moving as he follows the melody.
Warm light from a single window, dust in the air.
Cinematic, medium shots alternating with close-ups on hands and faces,
shallow depth of field, film grain. No text, no watermark.
```

### 1-C 「选错」· 文化失传

```
The same room, decades later, in flat daylight. Empty. The instruments lean
against the wall under a layer of dust — a rawap with slack strings, a drum
with a torn skin. A chair sits where the singer sat. Wind moves grit across
the floor through an open door.
The camera drifts slowly past each instrument and stops on the empty chair.
No people. No music.
Cinematic, desaturated, flat overcast light, film grain.
No text, no watermark, no people.
```

### 1-D 「成功案例」· 当代传承人

```
A modern small theatre or arts school in Xinjiang. A middle-aged master
musician sits facing a semicircle of about eight teenage students, each holding
a rawap or a satar. He leans over one student, adjusts the angle of the
instrument in her hands, then nods and steps back. They begin to play together.
Afternoon light through tall windows.
Cinematic, warm daylight, medium and close shots, shallow depth of field,
film grain. No text, no watermark.
```

## 场景二 · 草原 · 藏族文化

### 2-A 「选择」用的环境镜头

```
An immense Tibetan plateau grassland under a wide sky. Low hills to the
horizon, a line of prayer flags on a rise, wind bending the grass in long
waves. A single figure stands in the middle distance with a walking staff,
facing away.
Cinematic extreme wide shot, very slow push-in, film grain, cool blue and
muted green. No text, no watermark.
```

### 2-B 「选对」· 文化活着

```
High plateau in late afternoon. A bard sits cross-legged on a flat stone,
wearing a conch-shell hat, chanting the Gesar epic with one hand raised.
Around him herders in thick coats sit on the grass, absolutely still,
listening. A young man crouches at the edge of the circle, recording on a
phone held low, careful not to interrupt.
Wind moves the prayer flags behind them. Snow peaks far off.
Cinematic, high-altitude light, rich colour, film grain, medium shots and
close-ups on faces. No text, no watermark.
```

### 2-C 「选错」· 文化失传

```
The same stone platform in winter, empty. Prayer flags bleached white and
torn, snapping hard in the wind. Thin snow on the stone where the bard sat.
No people anywhere on the plain. The wind is the only movement.
Camera orbits the empty platform slowly and settles; the snow peaks behind do
not move.
Cinematic, cold desaturated light, film grain.
No text, no watermark, no people.
```

### 2-D 「成功案例」· 当代传承人

```
A bright classroom or cultural centre on the plateau. A middle-aged bard in a
conch-shell hat sits on a low platform facing about fifteen students, most of
them young, some in modern jackets. He chants a line; the students repeat it
back in unison. One student's notebook is open on the desk, writing as he
listens.
Sunlight through the windows, dust in the air.
Cinematic, natural daylight, medium shots and close-ups, film grain.
No text, no watermark.
```
